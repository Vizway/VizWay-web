## Purpose and intended use

VizOS aims to provide a controlled foundation for software development and system administration.

## Current development

VizOS is an active internal technology project and continues to evolve. It uses declarative configuration in NixOS to define how installations and working environments can be reproduced. The work also covers virtualization and isolated working environments.

## Future possibilities

The aim is to make it easier to reproduce and maintain environments for a wider range of development and system administration tasks.
