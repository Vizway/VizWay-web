## Purpose and intended use

Viz AI Core is a concept for a platform where organizations could build AI solutions for their own work.

## Current development

The project is at the concept stage. The platform's capabilities have yet to be verified.

## Future possibilities

The ambition is to develop a specialized AI core that can coordinate multiple AI agents and control their access to tools and external systems. Such a platform could be adapted to the needs of different organizations.
