## Purpose and intended use

Bygga Välstånd (Building Prosperity) is intended to help users analyze financial opportunities and weigh alternatives against long-term goals.

## Current development

The project is in development. It focuses on financial analysis and support for evaluating different strategies.

## Future possibilities

The aim is to make it easier to compare long-term options and understand the assumptions behind a decision.
