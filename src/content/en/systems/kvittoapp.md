## Purpose and intended use

The project aims to simplify the steps from a photograph of a receipt or an invoice to supporting documentation for bookkeeping.

## Current development

### Part 1 – KvittoApp

The mobile app is designed around a simple workflow. The idea is to photograph receipts or invoices, create a PDF and email it to the intended recipients.

### Part 2 – OCR → SIE

Work on OCR → SIE has begun. This part is still in development.

## Future possibilities

The goal for part 2 is to use optical character recognition (OCR) to read receipts and invoices, identify financial details and draft supporting documentation for bookkeeping. Users should be able to review the data and any proposed accounting entries before exporting them in SIE format for further bookkeeping.
