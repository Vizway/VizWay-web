## Purpose and intended use

BOS is intended to help organizations plan their work, put a structure in place and track progress.

## Current development

BOS is in development. The work focuses on bringing together support for planning, organizational structure, operations management and follow-up.

## Future possibilities

The aim is to make it easier to connect organizational goals with planning and follow-up. This would provide a shared basis for decisions when work needs to change.
